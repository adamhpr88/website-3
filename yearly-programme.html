Yearly lawn-care programme

Regular care and seasonal treatments to keep your lawn healthy, dense and looking its best.

What the programme includes

  • Weekly lawn cuts throughout the main growing season
  • A summertime mini-renovation
  • Light scarification
  • Overseeding
  • Fertiliser applications
  • Appropriate lawn treatments and sprays
  • Pest and disease monitoring and management

Regular cutting keeps the grass dense and tidy. Seasonal treatments, from fertiliser to overseeding and light scarification, feed and renew the lawn, while ongoing monitoring helps catch pests and diseases early.

Prices depend on the size and requirements of each job. Every lawn is different, so quotes are tailored to the work required.

You do not need to be home during your scheduled lawn cut. Simply ensure safe access to your garden.
